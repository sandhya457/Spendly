About Spendly

Spendly is a simple expense and budget tracking website designed to help students and everyday users manage money with less stress.

1
Track everything in one place

Record expenses, set budgets, organize categories, and view reports without switching apps.

2
Make smarter financial decisions

Understand your spending habits through summaries, charts, and category breakdowns.

3
Stay focused on your goals

Build better habits and move closer to your saving targets every month.

Why users choose Spendly

We built Spendly to feel simple, clean, and easy to understand. You don’t need complicated accounting knowledge to start managing money better.

Clean dashboard

See budget, expenses, reports, and goals from one place.

Fast setup

Create your account and begin tracking expenses in minutes.

Student-friendly design

Designed to be simple enough for daily personal finance use.
